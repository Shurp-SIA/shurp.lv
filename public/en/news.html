Article 26 August 2026

What is carpooling?

Carpooling is simple — people heading the same way travel together and share the cost of the trip.

Riga, Latvia — 26 August 2026. Someone is already driving to Riga, Liepāja, Valmiera or Jelgava. Others need to get in the same direction. Some cars have empty seats; some people do not have a car of their own. Why not ride together and share the cost?

The idea of carpooling is not new around the world, but in Latvia it is still little known and mostly informal — among friends, in Facebook groups or scattered chat threads. Shurp makes that process simpler and open to everyone by bringing it into one free app.

Not a new trip — using a trip that is already happening

Carpooling is not a taxi and not carsharing. It is based on a trip the driver would take anyway, even without a passenger.

If someone is driving from Riga to Liepāja on Friday and has three empty seats, they can offer them to others. Someone who needs to get to Liepāja can find that ride and join. Driver and passenger travel together, and the road costs are shared.

Both sides gain — the driver can lower their trip costs, and the passenger gets a way to reach their destination without their own car.

Carpooling already happens — often chaotically

Carpooling is not new in Latvia. People have been finding each other for years in Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats and friend circles.

“Is anyone driving from Riga to Valmiera on Friday?”
“Maybe someone is going to Liepāja with one free seat?”

Questions like these are nothing unusual here. The problem is that the information sits in different groups and threads, and finding the right ride is not always easy. Shurp’s idea is to make that simpler — find or offer a ride in one place.

Why ride alone when you can ride together?

Every day, cars on Latvian roads travel with empty seats. At the same time, people are looking for a way to get to work, home, studies, an event or another city. Carpooling connects those two situations.

It might be a student going home for the weekend. Someone who commutes to another city every day. A family without a car on a particular day. Or simply someone who does not want to pay for an expensive taxi. If someone is already going your way, there is no need to create another trip.

Lower costs for both sides

The essence of carpooling is sharing costs — not carrying passengers for profit. The driver takes their planned trip, and passengers help cover part of the expenses. That makes using the car more affordable, while the passenger gets another way to get around.

Shurp is built exactly for this kind of non-commercial carpooling — share the ride, share the tank.

And the benefit is not only for your wallet

When several people ride in one car, you do not need several cars. That means fewer empty seats, fewer cars on the road and lighter traffic. Fuel use and trip-related emissions are also spread across more people.

The more people who are already heading the same way choose to ride together, the more empty seats in cars get used.

Carpooling is not only a last resort

Shurp’s goal is for carpooling to become a normal everyday travel choice. Not only when the bus is cancelled. Not only when the public transport timetable does not fit. And not only when a car suddenly is not available.

If people already travel the same directions every day, carpooling can simply become one more usual way to get around.

Shurp makes it simpler

Shurp lets you search for a ride or offer a free seat in your car. Today you can be a passenger; tomorrow — a driver. The app helps you find people going the same way, get in touch and agree the trip. All of it — in one free app.

Carpooling is simple: people are going the same way, so they ride together. Why ride alone when you can ride together — and cheaper?

Press release 19 June 2026

Shurp — Latvia’s free carpooling app is here

A new app built in Latvia that helps people ride together. Shurp lets you find or offer rides across Latvia in one place — simply share the road, share the tank.

Riga, Latvia — 19 June 2026. Shurp SIA (Reg. No. LV40203709369) launches Shurp — a free carpooling app built in Latvia.

The idea is simple: if you are already going somewhere, why not take along someone heading the same way? And if you do not have a car, you can find a ride with others.

Shurp is not a taxi. It is a platform for people who are already on the road and want to travel together. Find or post a ride, chat in the app, and agree the trip and how you share the costs.

Why ride together?

  • Save money — share the cost of the trip instead of driving alone or paying taxi rates.
  • Use empty seats — if you are going anyway, take others along and split the road costs.
  • Meet people — a shared journey can also be a good way to meet someone new.
  • Fewer empty seats, fewer cars — more people in one car means fewer unnecessary trips.

The whole trip in one place

Shurp lets you post a ride or find a route that fits in Riga, Pieriga and across Latvia. After a ride is confirmed, you can message the others in the in-app chat, check profiles and reviews, and use optional document verification.

Shurp takes no commission on cost-sharing between users.

The app is brand new, and its value comes from the people who use it. So we invite you to join, publish your routes, and invite friends, colleagues, neighbours and others you already tend to travel with in the same direction.

Download Shurp and try it today.
