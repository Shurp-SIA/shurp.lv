Short explainer videos and answers.

Shurp is Latvia's free carpooling* app. Not a taxi — just people riding together.
Find or add a ride. Arrange it. Travel and settle in person.
Not only Riga and Pieriga. Shurp is available to everyone in Latvia.

When to ride with Shurp?

  • When the bus or train does not run at the right time or without a transfer
  • When carsharing is too expensive for an intercity trip
  • When a taxi is expensive or not available at all
  • When you already drive daily and want to save by splitting road costs

What sets Shurp apart

  • Zero commission — no fees for posting or requesting a ride
  • Everything in one place — search, requests, chat and every direction in your pocket
  • Real people — cost-sharing between real people
  • This is NOT a taxi — cost-sharing, not commercial transport
  • Safe — registered users only, public profiles, reviews and ID validation

*Carpooling is when we travel together in the same direction and split the road costs.

If you drive with empty seats — from Riga to Liepaja, Daugavpils, Jelgava, or just to work in Riga-area or anywhere else in Latvia — add a ride and share fuel costs. Your car, your rules.

Add and repeat

  • Enter from, to and when — intercity and daily city routes too
  • Recurring rides — pick weekdays and with one tap your rides stay on Shurp
  • Seats and price — you set the price and flexibility; you can also take friends or neighbours for free
  • Before publishing, add a car and complete your profile

Requests and messaging

  • Share the link right away — chats, socials, colleagues, neighbours
  • Accept or decline a request after seeing the profile and reviews
  • After confirmation — chat or use WhatsApp
  • After the trip leave a rating — that is how a trusted carpooling community grows

Search for cheap rides in Riga and Latvia when public transport does not fit: early mornings, late evenings, when a transfer-free bus or train trip does not work, or when you simply want a more comfortable ride. Shurp shows people already going your way.

Find and request

  • Search by filling: From, To and When — (you can also leave time empty and search «Anytime»)
  • Tip: enter only the destination if timing does not matter, then hit search
  • Filter «Verified only» if you want extra trust
  • One tap can send a request to multiple drivers

If nothing turns up

  • Create a ride alert — a reminder when someone posts the same route
  • Share the search — maybe a colleague or friend is heading to Ventspils, Bauska, Valmiera or Rezekne…
  • Cheaper and more flexible than carsharing if you do not need the whole car to yourself

In carpooling, trust is everything. A good profile helps you connect faster — for daily work trips and intercity routes.

Fill in your profile

  • Name + a short «About you»
  • Phone — private; visible only to your confirmed ride partner
  • My Garage — make, model, fuel, seats
  • Complete document validation to build trust with other users
  • Public and private profiles are separate — you control what others see

Carpooling in Latvia still often lives in chat groups — Shurp can bring them together and simplify them. Share right from Shurp and the ride stays in the app, while the link goes to people who already know you — colleagues, neighbours, chat mates.

Where to share

  • Right after adding a ride — one button
  • From the ride card
  • From empty search results — «Hey, maybe you are going that way?»

Why it works

  • Friends respond faster and already trust you
  • You show you are a real person with a real route
  • Sharing gives you a better chance of finding a ride mate

Safe carpooling starts with information. Shurp offers several trust layers — before you choose to ride together.

Before the trip

  • Profiles — name, photo, bio and rating
  • Reviews — what others said after shared trips
  • Document validation — GDPR-safe, optional, with a verified badge on your profile

On the way and after

  • Chat after confirmation; WhatsApp — meet-up and location
  • Phone is never public — matched partner only
  • Report — safety, scam, no-show, and more
  • Reports are reviewed; rule-breakers lose access to Shurp
