Every commute counts. Not just shared rides

Shurp helps organisations understand how their employees travel to work. From solo commutes to shared journeys, real-world travel and vehicle data provides the foundation for measuring commuting emissions, identifying savings and supporting Scope 3, Category 7 reporting.

Give your employees an easier way to share their commute while giving your organisation measurable insight into workplace travel. Shurp connects colleagues travelling in the same direction, helps reduce single-occupancy journeys and provides clear reports on shared kilometres, estimated CO₂ savings and commuting costs.
