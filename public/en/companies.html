Privacy and trust

Respectful of data. Clear legal model

Employee control, non-commercial carpooling and verified identity

Shurp is built so employees control what they share with their employer, while businesses get clear mobility insights without a surveillance feel.

Your data. Your choice Rides are not automatically visible to the employer — employees choose what to share.

Data is shared only with the employee's consent. Linking an account to an organisation does not, by itself, disclose ride details.

  1. 1

    Employee links the company

    The organisation does not see rides until the employee marks them separately.

  2. 2

    Employee decides whether to share it

    Before marking a ride, the app shows what the organisation will see for that trip.

  3. 3

    Only selected rides contribute to reports

    Unmarked rides and personal travel are not visible to the employer.

  • Organisations do not receive precise live location.
  • Employees can stop marking future rides or unlink their account.
  • Already delivered reports are not automatically deleted after unlinking.

Real carpooling. Not commercial passenger transport A non-commercial model built on the framework set out in applicable law.

Shurp is built around non-commercial carpooling. Drivers share journeys they already plan to make, while passengers contribute a proportional share of the travel costs. The platform’s operating concept is built on the framework set out in applicable law and on non-commercial carpooling principles.

  • Drivers must not make a profit from transporting passengers.
  • Shurp is not a taxi company and not a commercial passenger transport provider.
  • Shurp does not process ride payments and does not take a commission on shared journey costs.
  • Settlements happen privately between journey participants.

Read the Terms of Use

Trust starts with verified identities Identity verification through Didit, an independent EU provider.

Shurp offers identity and document verification through Didit, an independent EU-based provider. A verification status is displayed on the user's profile, helping people make more informed decisions before sharing a ride.

  • Drivers: driver's licence verification.
  • Passengers: voluntary identity-document verification.
  • Document scanning and biometric comparison take place in Didit's environment; Shurp stores the verification status and an encrypted identifier.
