Pressure points Shurp relieves

Five workplace problems — one connected mobility habit

Same corridor

Site access

Industrial parks, airports and logistics hubs are hard to reach. Matching people on the same corridor widens who can take the job.

Employee costs

Fuel and wear hit the same people every morning. Splitting the ride is a tangible benefit they feel in the first week — not only at year-end.

More parking

Parking load

Every empty seat is a bay you did not have to build, rent or clear in winter. Shared arrivals cut peak occupancy without new asphalt.

CO₂ savings

Climate & CO₂ savings

Shared rides cut estimated commute emissions and solo kilometres. Put those CO₂ and distance savings next to your ESG targets — including Scope 3 Category 7.

Verified trips

Company rides, trip logs & allowances

Manage work-related rides and frequent corridors in one place. Use real ride data for company-car trip logs, mileage records and driving allowances — not spreadsheet estimates.

How it works

Simple setup, ready in seconds

From workplace invite to shared rides and clear results

  1. 1

    Connect the workplace

    Invite employees with a simple QR or email link. Shifts and work rides are ready to match.

  2. 2

    Match and share

    People find others on the same route and ride together. Cost is split in the app.

  3. 3

    See the result

    Shared kilometres, estimated CO₂ and cost savings, and less pressure on parking — in reports you can actually use.

Not only colleagues

Employees also get access to the wider Shurp network — neighbours and others heading the same way. More matches, greener everyday habits, beyond the office walls.

A key differentiator

Every commute counts. Not just shared rides

Shurp helps organisations understand how their employees travel to work. From solo commutes to shared journeys, real-world travel and vehicle data provides the foundation for measuring commuting emissions, identifying savings and supporting Scope 3, Category 7 reporting — at workplace aggregate level, with privacy built in.
