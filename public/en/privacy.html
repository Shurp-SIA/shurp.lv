Privacy Policy: Shurp

Last updated: May 2026

1. Our Philosophy: Less is Safer.

We do not collect data we do not need, and we do not store it longer than necessary.

Your privacy is built into our app architecture.

2. Who is the Data Controller?

Your personal data controller is SIA Shurp, Reg. No. LV40203709369, Email for privacy inquiries:

3. What Data Do We Process?

  • Public profile (visible to all users): Name, profile photo, "About me" text description, ride preferences (music, smoking, etc.), vehicle make and model (drivers only), verification status, and "Supporter" badge. Profile photos are stored in Supabase Storage (Ireland, EU) with a public URL so other users can see them on profiles and in ride results.
  • Reviews. After a ride you may leave a rating (1–5 stars) and a comment about another participant. These ratings are publicly visible on the other user's profile together with your name and profile photo. Ratings remain available even after you delete your account, but they are anonymized.
  • Private data (visible only to you): Your email address is used for authentication and communication (for example, password reset). Your password is stored in encrypted form and is not visible to anyone, including Shurp administration.
  • Data for confirmed ride participants (disclosed only after the ride is confirmed): Full name and phone number. The phone number is disclosed securely in the app so ride participants can contact each other. After the ride, this data is not publicly available.
  • Location data: The app processes only the start points, destinations, and routes you specify yourself. There is no background tracking. If you use your device's location, that information is used only to determine a specific address. If you create a ride alert, your searched route (start–destination) is stored until you delete it or turn it off.
  • Technical data: Device IP address, operating system version, and error reports — for system maintenance.
  • Notification data: Your device's unique notification identifier (FCM token) — required to send push notifications. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Help section: If you contact support, we store your request, email, and technical information (for example, device type) so we can respond.
  • Optional website analytics: When enabled, website analytics starts only after you select “Allow”. We record page and action categories (such as page views, navigation, and app-store clicks), language, and a random identifier that lasts only for the current page. We do not collect search text, precise locations, page query strings, account identifiers, or form contents for this analytics. Events are kept for up to 90 days. Only your consent preference is saved in your browser; no persistent visitor identifier is used. You can decline or withdraw consent using “Analytics settings” on the website. Declining does not affect the service. Website clicks do not establish app installs or completed bookings.
  • Product usage analytics: Shurp processes first-party usage events to understand how the Platform works overall (for example, search, empty result, opening a ride card, request, creating an alert, publishing or sharing a ride). Data may include a pseudonymized account/session identifier, app version, platform, role, and approximate corridor. Names, phone numbers, messages, and precise addresses are not stored; there is no screen recording or background GPS tracking.

4. Verification Data (Identity Confirmation)

To promote safety and mutual trust in the community, Shurp offers identity verification using an independent service provider based in the European Union, Didit (Didit Solutions S.L.).

  • Standards compliance: Didit is a GDPR-compliant platform registered in the European Union that provides digital processing of identity documents in line with the core principles of the eIDAS regulation.
  • Verification process: For drivers: driver's licence check to obtain verified status. For passengers: voluntary identity document check (ID card or passport).
  • How we process this data: Document scanning, data reading, and biometric comparison take place in Didit's secure environment. Only the final result is stored in Shurp's database: "Verified: Yes/No" and a unique encrypted identifier.
  • Data storage and access: We do not store document images, serial numbers, or personal identification numbers on our servers. Shurp administration accesses Didit data only in exceptional cases: investigating security incidents, resolving legal disputes, fixing errors, or at your direct request.

5. Communication Between Users.

After a ride is confirmed, participants can communicate in two ways:

  • Shurp in-app chat — message content is stored on our servers (Supabase, EU) for up to 90 days after the ride for safety and dispute resolution. After that, content is deleted, but metadata (who, when, and with whom you communicated) is kept for 1 more year.
  • WhatsApp handoff — at the user's own choice. In that case, communication takes place on Meta's servers in accordance with WhatsApp's privacy policy, and Shurp is not involved.

6. Data Security.

  • Access restriction: Access to user verification data on the third-party platform (Didit) is limited to authorized Shurp administrators only.
  • Data segregation: Your public profile never displays private data — only the fact of verification.
  • Encryption: All communication between Shurp, Didit, and your device is encrypted (TLS/SSL).

7. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Data processing is based on:

  • Contract performance: To provide the service and connect you with other users.
  • Legitimate interests: Ensuring security, preventing fraud, and improving the service, including pseudonymized product usage analytics and building aggregated funnel/corridor metrics to improve matching, app performance, and user experience.
  • Consent: When you grant access to device features (for example, camera or location).
  • Legal obligation: Ride data (route, price, date) is stored for 5 years in accordance with Latvian tax and transport law requirements. Data is linked to an anonymous user identifier — not your name or contact information. When you delete your account, this identifier is also permanently removed.

8. How Long Do We Store Data?

We do not store data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Active rides: stored for up to 10 days after the ride, then moved to the archive.
  • Ride archive: stored for 5 years in accordance with legal requirements, then automatically deleted.
  • Chat content: deleted 90 days after the ride. Technical metadata (who messaged whom and when) — after 1 year.
  • User complaints: anonymized after they expire, retaining only statistical data.
  • When you delete your account: all personal data is permanently deleted. Only anonymous ride data remains in the ride archive, with no link to your identity.
  • Product usage analytics events: stored for a limited time (up to 90 days). After that, or when the account is deleted, we delete account-linked events; aggregated, non-personally-identifying statistics (for example, search/request counts) may be retained.

9. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell data to third parties and never pass your personal data to advertisers.

  • Advertising: Shurp does not currently show ads in the app. If advertising is introduced in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before showing ads in accordance with GDPR and the ePrivacy rules (for example, iOS App Tracking Transparency and consent dialogs in Europe).

Data may be shared only with:

  • Ride participants - name and phone number only after the ride is confirmed. Communication primarily takes place in Shurp's in-app chat; at the user's choice it may continue in the WhatsApp app (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited), which is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Technical partners: Supabase Inc. (server infrastructure, authentication, database and file storage, hosted in Ireland, EU); Didit Solutions S.L. (Spain, EU — identity verification); Google Ireland Limited (push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging); Apple Inc. / Google LLC (if you sign in with Apple ID or a Google account, partners provide us with your email address and, for Google, your name and a link to your profile photo stored on Google's servers. Apple App Store / Google Play (for voluntary "Supporter" payments — Shurp does not receive or store payment card data).
  • Aggregated statistics for partners: Shurp may share with partners, sponsors, or municipalities only aggregated, non-personally-identifying metrics, for example search, empty-result, or request counts in a given corridor and period. Shurp does not provide advertisers with individual searches, user lists, profile data, or other personal data for marketing profiling.

All technical partners operate under Data Processing Agreements (DPA) and GDPR.

All data is encrypted both in transit (TLS) and at rest.

10. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access: request at any time what data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary.
  • Restriction: request restriction of data processing.
  • Data portability: request a copy of your data for download or transfer to another service.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw previously given consent at any time.

Send requests to . We will respond within 30 days.

If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate (www.dvi.gov.lv).
