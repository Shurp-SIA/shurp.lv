How does it work?

Ride with the pack

Shurp connects people going the same way — not a taxi, but everyday people sharing road costs. Simpler than chat groups, everything in one place.

Zero commissions

Sign up and use it safely for free. No commissions — every cent stays with you and your travel buddy.

As easy as it looks

Search your route, message, and settle fuel when you meet.

Shurp is a cost-sharing platform, not a commercial transport service. All payments happen directly between users.

Searching rides

How to find a ride

  • Enter From, To, and When
  • Time is optional
  • Check the profile and tap Request ride

After confirmation

Chat or use WhatsApp. Rides end automatically — then leave a rating.

Tips if nothing turns up

  • Search only To if timing does not matter
  • Turn on route alerts
  • Share with friends in chats and socials
Driving with Shurp

Add a ride

Tap Add ride, enter where from, where to, and when. You can set a recurring ride by weekdays so you never have to post the same trip again.

Seats, price and sharing

Choose free seats and price (including free). Share with one tap in chats and socials.

Requests and chat

Several passengers can request at once — you approve based on profile and reviews. Chat, WhatsApp, send a photo or location. After the trip, leave a rating.

Make sure notifications are on — that is how you hear about new requests.

Safety and trust

1. Profiles

Check the name and photo — it might be someone you already know.

2. Reviews

See how others rated riding with this person before you go.

3. Document verification

GDPR-safe identity verification is optional, but we recommend it — it shows you are a real person.

4. Chat and reporting

In-app chat and WhatsApp coordination. If something is not OK — report the user. All reports are reviewed, and rule-breakers lose access.

Carpooling is only as good as its community — and we build that community ourselves.

Verification & safety
How does driver verification work?
We use Didit ID to verify the driver's licence and identity. After verification, drivers get a badge on their profile. We don't store document numbers, only an encrypted verification status.
Do passengers need to verify?
Passengers don't need ID verification to search for rides. For drivers' peace of mind and a safer community, we also recommend voluntary ID verification for passengers.
What if something goes wrong during a ride?
In an emergency, always contact emergency services. After the trip, use the Report function in the app.
Data privacy
What data does Shurp collect?
Only what's necessary: your name, phone number, profile photo (optional), and route data for matching. We never track your location in the background.
Can other users see my personal data?
Your public profile shows only your first name, photo, car info, and rating. Last name and phone are shared only with your confirmed ride partner.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account. Personal data is permanently deleted. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
Is Shurp free to use?
No hidden catches?
Exactly — no commissions or middlemen. The app is free, and there are no fees for posting rides. 100% of the ride cost stays with the driver.
Does Shurp take a cut from the ride?
Zero. Shurp doesn't process payments — all settlements happen directly between driver and passenger when you meet.
How can I support the project?
Rate the app on the App Store or Play Store, share your experience on socials, or optionally tip under Support project in your profile.
Car & Profile management
What should I fill in on my profile?
Your name and a short bio. A phone number helps WhatsApp handoff. Document verification is optional but builds trust.
Can I add more than one car?
Sure. Add multiple cars and pick the one you need for each ride. Your garage is in profile settings and the Drive section.
Can I edit a ride after posting?
Yes, as long as there are no active bookings. Tap the ride and edit any time.
How do I change a booked ride?
Booked rides can't be edited anymore. Message your passenger(s) in chat if something needs to change.
How do I delete a booked ride?
Let passengers know, remove them one by one in the app — then you can edit or delete the ride.
Booking & Requests
What if I need to cancel a ride?
Use in-app chat to let your travel buddy know as soon as you can. Cancelling isn't a disaster, but it's great if you give advance notice.
What if someone doesn't show up?
We call that "ghosting" — a serious community violation. Frequent no-shows lower your rating.
How do I pay for fuel?
All payments happen directly between you and your travel buddy when you meet. See our Terms of Use for full details.
Ratings & Community
How does the rating system work?
After each completed trip, both driver and passenger can rate the ride. Ratings and comments are visible on each user's profile.
What happens if I get a bad rating?
One bad rating won't sink you — they even out over time. Consistently low ratings may affect your matching opportunities.
How do I report if something went wrong?
Use the Report button on the user's profile or in the message screen. All reports are reviewed.
