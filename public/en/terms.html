Shurp Rules in Brief (TL;DR)

  • Shurp is not a taxi. Drivers publish trips they already plan to take. Passengers join an already planned trip and cover a proportional share of travel costs.
  • No profit. Drivers receive only reimbursement for travel expenses (fuel, depreciation).
  • No commissions. Shurp does not charge fees on trip payments.
  • Be reliable. If plans change — give timely notice. "Ghosting" is not respected here.
  • Respect each other's space. Agree on luggage, pets, or breaks before confirming a trip.
  • Safety first. Only real profiles and genuine identity.
  • Emergency services first. Shurp is not 112. If something happens — call emergency services immediately!

Shurp is not liable for payment delays, refusals, or disputes relating to cost-sharing for trips, and Users are themselves responsible for fulfilling their tax obligations.

3.2. Non-commercial nature: In accordance with the Republic of Latvia and general European Union carpooling principles, the Driver may not profit from a trip. A Passenger's contribution covers only a proportional share of the trip's direct costs (fuel, road tolls, parking) and variable operating costs (vehicle wear and tear). For a trip to be classified as non-commercial cost-sharing, the total amount received from all Passengers must at no time exceed the total operating costs of the specific route. The Driver may not retain a financial surplus (profit) from the trip.

3.3. Algorithm control: To prevent the possibility of profit-making, the Platform's algorithm automatically calculates and sets the maximum cost-sharing amount for each trip. The Driver may not request from Passengers an amount exceeding this platform-set limit.

3.4. Private settlements: Users independently agree among themselves on the method and timing of payment (for example, cash or private bank transfer).
